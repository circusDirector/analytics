Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What we access

When one of our own TikTok accounts connects to Clip Stats, we receive, with that account's permission: its username, display name and profile picture; its follower, following, like and video counts; and the list of its public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

What we do with it

We store these figures in our internal reporting database to track how our own videos perform over time. We do not sell, share or publish this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we do not access

We do not access messages, private videos, drafts, or any data about other TikTok users.

Retention and removal

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After that, no new data is collected for that account.
